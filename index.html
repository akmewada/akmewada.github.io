Professional Bio

Dr. Arvind Mewada is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NIT Calicut, Kerala. His research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP), with particular expertise in fake review detection, sentiment analysis, and transformer-based models for opinion spam analysis.

His doctoral research at MNNIT Allahabad presented an integrated framework for detecting deceptive reviews, including hybrid approaches for individual and group spammer detection, and aspect-based sentiment analysis. He has also contributed significantly to computer vision applications in medical imaging and blockchain-based privacy frameworks.
