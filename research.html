Research Highlights

A chronological record of breakthroughs in NLP, fake review detection, machine learning, and computer vision.

Major Milestone

ConvRoBERTa: Advanced Fake Review Detection

Developed ConvRoBERTa, a novel model combining scaled dot-product attention with sequential (textual) and non-sequential (behavioral and sentiment polarity) signals for detecting fake reviews. Demonstrated strong performance on the Yelp dataset compared to traditional single-representation methods.

science IEEE Transactions on AI, 2026

2025

NRWalk2Vec-HIN: Spammer Group Detection

Constructed a heterogeneous information network of users, reviews, timestamps, and ratings, employing NRWalk2Vec embeddings and Gaussian fuzzy C-Means clustering with entropy-driven weights to detect coordinated spammer groups effectively on social media.

link The Journal of Supercomputing, 2024

SUH-AIFRD: Hybrid Fake Reviewer Detection

Proposed a self-training-based hybrid approach combining content-based, behavioral, temporal, and relational features to improve detection accuracy of individual fake reviewers, even in scenarios with limited labeled data.

SA-ASBA: Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis

Developed a hybrid ABSA model incorporating transformer-based attention, dynamic word embeddings, and XGBoost classification for precise sentiment analysis across multiple product aspects.
